## Discovery and access

The inforegs project and archive supplied original OMB and NIST material, legacy baseline/template files, and preserved FedRAMP content. FedRAMP MCP supplied status, rules, RFC outcomes and complete discussion context. MCP imports were manually synchronized through November 6, 2026, with rules version 2026.11.02.01; current rule pages were fetched live on November 11. Video coverage was partial, and no conclusion relies on unreviewed video transcripts.

External discovery used web search, official and historical SitesUSA URLs, government-authored documents preserved by third parties, and Internet Archive CDX/replay. Google was explicitly attempted: browser access was unavailable (host locked), and direct Google requests returned an interactive challenge. No successful Google search is claimed. Archive requests initially returned errors; sequential `if_` replay recovered the 2020 draft and February 2024 scanning PDF. Failed requests are not treated as evidence that a document never existed.

Current source links may change. Retained government originals and SHA-256 values provide a way to inspect the exact historical editions used. Public-comment originals remain linked rather than copied wholesale.
